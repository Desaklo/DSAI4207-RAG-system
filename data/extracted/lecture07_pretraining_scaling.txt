Who Is Adam?

Researcher Reviewer
A long-running joke in the machine-learning community. This dialogue is fictional.

Adam = Adaptive Moment Estimation
An optimization algorithm — not a PhD student who trained everyone’s models.

Memory 1 · Direction
SGD sees the current slope
First moment m: a running average
of the signed gradients
Momentum + remembers directions
Memory 2 · Scale
Adam + remembers the scale
Second moment v: a running average
of the squared gradients

Adam has two memories: one for the direction of recent gradients, one for their scale.
Kingma & Ba, Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization (arXiv 2014; ICLR 2015).
