04 / 17

Ambiguity and missing information

Request fragment Expected interpretation

Atlas from 02:00 to 03:00 The explicit two-digit times are valid.

Atlas from 2 to 3 Both times are null under the lab policy.

Atlas starting at 14:00 Keep the start. The end is null.

Atlas at 14:00 for one hour The end remains null. Do not calculate it.

Assess each field independently. Keep clear information when another field is unresolved.
